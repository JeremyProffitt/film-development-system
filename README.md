# film-development-system

Automating black & white and C-41 film processing around a Paterson Super System 4
tank: how to fill and drain it quickly, how to route the chemistry, and a motorised
tumbler that gives the tank real inversion agitation.

Part of a set of analog darkroom projects alongside
[film-dryer](https://github.com/JeremyProffitt/film-dryer) (filtered air system for film drying boxes)
and [film-holders](https://github.com/JeremyProffitt/film-holders).

## Repository layout

```
research/   analysis and drawing sets for the tank itself
tumbler/    the motorised tank tumbler: parametric model, STLs, drawings
```

Every PDF here is generated by the script beside it. Nothing is hand-drawn, and
every dimension quoted on a tumbler sheet is measured off the exported STL rather
than taken from the design intent.

### research/

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `tank_drawing_set.py` / `.pdf` | 6 sheets. The Super System 4 range and a dimensioned drawing of every component of the Multi-Reel 3: tank body, centre column, light-trap funnel, lid, agitator rod. |
| `fill_and_drain_study.py` / `.pdf` | 15 sheets. Why the stock tank fills slowly, every route to filling and draining it faster, tilt geometry, pump vs gravity, fluid architecture, carry-over budget, and per-process sequences for B&W and C-41. |

```bash
cd research && python fill_and_drain_study.py
```

### tumbler/

A drum lying on its side with a bore running through it at right angles. The tank
lives in that bore, so one turn of the drum is one full inversion of the tank.
The first 34 mm at each open end carries square grooves on its **inner** wall;
eight TT motors — four per end, 90° apart — sit inside the bore with their wheels
pressing **outward** against those grooves.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `model.py` | parametric model; writes `stl/` |
| `drawings.py` | 4-sheet drawing set; writes `tumbler_drawings.pdf` |
| `doc_framework.py` | shared A3 sheet helpers |
| `render_stl.py` | headless multi-view STL renderer (no OpenGL) |

```bash
cd tumbler && python model.py && python drawings.py
```

Key numbers: drum Ø270 × 198, bore Ø250, tank bore Ø106, 78 grooves at 10.07 mm
pitch, drive ratio 0.252 giving **30 / 47 / 63 inversions per minute** at 3 / 4.5 / 6 V,
about 2.5 N·m at the drum. Every part fits a Bambu H2D with 5 mm clearance per axis.

Bought parts: 8 × [Adafruit 3777](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3777) TT motors,
8 × [Adafruit 3766](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3766) wheels, 6 V 10 A supply.

## Provenance, and what to check before cutting metal

**Paterson publishes no dimensional data for the Super System 4 range.** No spec
sheets, no drawings — this was checked against the manufacturer and six retailers.
The tank dimensions used throughout are a reconstruction built from a small number
of sourced figures:

- internal Ø95 mm (3.75 in), and Multi-Reel 5 internal depths of 229 mm to the
  funnel and 279 mm to the lid — vendor spec Q&A
- retail packaging heights 127 / 165 / 294 / 442 mm for PTP114 / 115 / 117 / 118
- fill volumes 290 ml per 135/126 roll, 370 ml per 127, 500 ml per 120/220

Everything else is derived from a 44 mm reel pitch that reproduces all four
published box heights to within 3 %, or reconstructed proportionally from product
photography. Sheets flag each figure as published, derived or reconstructed.

Before you drill, print or buy fittings: **measure the real tank**. In particular
the tumbler assumes the tank lid rim is larger than Ø106 so the tank cannot pass
through the drum bore, and the groove pitch assumes the 3766 tread block spacing,
which Adafruit does not publish.

## Known weaknesses

Documented on the sheets rather than hidden: eight friction-coupled TT motors will
fight each other at ±10 % speed spread; there are no bearings, so the tyres are the
bearings and are a consumable; the drive is open loop, so the tank parks wherever
it stops; and the drum as modelled needs splitting on the plane containing both
axes to print the cross-bore without support.
